How to read the audit

Targets are engineering decisions derived from the researched workflows. FormWeb grades come from source tracing and observed checks listed in the release verification record. Local publishing, basic CSS effects and optional agent access retain their limits instead of borrowing credit from managed infrastructure, timeline animation or team permissions.
